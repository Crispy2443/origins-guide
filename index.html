Grason • Luke • Carter

We got this. Time to free Maxis and Samantha.

1

Obtain & Upgrade All Elemental Staffs

2

Place the Staffs in the Robots

  • Lightning Staff → Robot at Spawn
  • Wind Staff → Middle Robot
  • Ice Staff → Church Robot
  • Fire Staff → Excavation Site Robot

Make sure all generators are on!

3

Obtain the G-Strikes

1. Tank Station → Grab glowing stone

2. Church → Put in holy water basin

3. Melee zombies to cleanse (turns silver)

4. Return without stepping in mud

5. Melee 20 more zombies until G-Strikes spawn

Once acquired:

  • One teammate presses red button in any robot (20 sec)
  • One throws G-Strikes on the stone tablet (near Lightning area)
4

Build Maxis Drone

Throw it near the pit (same spot as G-Strikes). Lots of Panzers incoming — Fire/Lightning Staff recommended.

5

Zombie Blood + Plane

Drink Zombie Blood from spawn reward chest.
Shoot the burning plane in the sky, then kill the pilot (only visible with blood).

6

Thunder Punch

Fill soul chests → Get Thunder Punch → Kill white mist zombies at bottom of Excavation Site (everyone needs upgraded fists).

7

Crazy Place Ritual

Place all staffs → Kill zombies until screen flashes white.
Achievement: Little Lost Girl

FINAL STEP - FREEDOM!!!

Fly the Maxis Drone into the Crazy Place

Everyone get in the middle → Look up → Press the button

"Father and Daughter finally meet again in Agartha..."
